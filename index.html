About the Doctor

Expert Hands. Honest Care.

Dr Rehan Sittar is a Consultant Orthopedic Surgeon (MBBS, FCPS) practising at Bashir Hospital, Railway Road, Gujrat. He treats the full spectrum of bone, joint and muscle problems — from shoulder pain and fractures to joint replacement, spine care and keyhole (arthroscopic) surgery.

His approach is simple: diagnose properly, explain your options clearly, and recommend surgery only when it is truly the right step for you.

  • MBBS, FCPS — Consultant Orthopedic Surgeon
  • Fracture management & complete trauma care — emergency available 24/7
  • Joint replacement — hip, knee & shoulder — plus arthroscopy for sports injuries (ACL, meniscus)
  • Spine surgery, pediatric orthopedics, and hand & foot disorders

Common Questions

Before You Visit

How do I book an appointment with Dr Rehan Sittar?

Call or WhatsApp 0347 1649065, or use the booking form on this page — your details open in WhatsApp, ready to send. The clinic will confirm your slot.

Where does Dr Rehan Sittar see patients?

At Bashir Hospital, Railway Road, Gujrat. Please call ahead to confirm the day's clinic timings before you travel.

I have shoulder pain. Do I need surgery?

Not always — many shoulder problems settle with medicines, rest and physiotherapy. Surgery is advised only when scans and examination show it is genuinely needed. Dr Rehan will assess you and explain every option honestly.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring any previous X-rays, MRI reports, prescriptions and a list of medicines you currently take. This helps the doctor understand your case fully in the first visit itself.
